iParking: Parking Management

Access Control and Real-Time Revenue Tracking

Project Synthesis

This comprehensive system allows for the efficient management of public and private parking lots. It controls the vehicle's entire cycle: from issuing an entry ticket with a barcode to the automatic rate calculation upon exit.

It offers robust tools to prevent capital loss through audits of canceled tickets, movement reports, and shift cash closures, ensuring a transparent and profitable operation.

System Interface

System Capabilities

  • Dynamic rate calculation (hourly, partial, daily, or long-term/pension).
  • Thermal ticket printing with 128/39 barcodes.
  • Long-term member (pensions) module with validity and monthly payment control.
  • Complete audit of lost tickets and manual cancellations.
  • Occupancy charts and peak-hour tracking for informed decision-making.

Tech Stack

VB 6.0 MS Access Crystal Reports

Control Modules

  • Real-Time Entries and Exits.
  • Cash Closure by Operator.
  • Long-Term/Pension Management.
