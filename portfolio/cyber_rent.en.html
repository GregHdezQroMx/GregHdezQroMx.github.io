Cyber Rent: Digital Center Control

Time and Service Management - Retail & Gaming

Project Synthesis

Cyber Rent is a comprehensive solution for managing internet cafes and equipment rental centers. The system automates time counting per device and the sale of additional products.

It allows for strict administrative control over daily income, managing printing services, rental time expiration alerts, and snack sales from a centralized console.

System Visualization

Technical Achievements

  • Remote station control via TCP/IP Socket communication.
  • Cross-selling module (integrated POS) for services and snacks.
  • Prepaid, postpaid, and time bonus management for frequent customers.
  • Profitability reports generation by station and time slot.

Tech Stack

VB 6.0 MS Access TCP/IP Sockets

Key Functions

  • Synchronization from admin PC to rental devices.
  • Inventory Control.
  • Sales of services and snacks.
